## 2. Invoicing Details Checklist

When issuing a GST Tax Invoice to a CA firm:
1. **Invoice Type:** Tax Invoice
2. **SAC / HSN Code:** `998314` (Information Technology software reproduction, distribution, and maintenance services)
3. **Place of Supply:**
   - Same state as you: **CGST 9% + SGST 9%**
   - Different state: **IGST 18%**
4. **Mandatory Fields on Invoice:**
   - Your Firm Name, Address, PAN & GSTIN
   - CA Firm Name, Address, PAN & GSTIN (vital for them to claim ITC)
   - Unique Invoice Number & Invoice Date
   - Description: *"WebRajya ERP — Lifetime Edition Software License (Includes 1-Year Statutory Updates & Maintenance)"*
   - Machine ID / License Key reference (e.g. `WR-HW-XXXX-...`)
   - Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM): No
