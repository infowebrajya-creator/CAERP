# WebRajya ERP — Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) Policy & SLA

**Standard AMC Fee:** ₹5,000 + 18% GST per annum  
**Applicable To:** Year 2 onwards for all WebRajya ERP Lifetime License holders  
**SAC Code:** 998314 (IT Support and Maintenance Services)

---

## 1. Scope of Covered AMC Services

When an active AMC is maintained, WebRajya Technologies provides:

### A. Statutory Tax & Regulatory Compliance Updates
- **GST Portal Changes:** Compatibility patches for updated JSON return schemas (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9).
- **Income Tax Portal Updates:** Revisions to ITR forms, tax slab calculators, and TDS rate charts as notified in the Union Budget each fiscal year.
- **Audit Documentation:** Updated statutory compliance deadlines and audit checklist templates.

### B. Software Enhancements & Bug Fixes
- Continuous performance optimizations and database query speed improvements.
- Security updates and PHP / MySQL compatibility patches.
- Access to all minor and major feature updates via the 1-click **In-App Auto-Updater**.

### C. Technical Support SLA
- **Email & Remote Desk Support:** Monday through Saturday (10:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST).
- **Priority Resolution:** Critical tax filing blockers resolved within 24 business hours.
- **Hardware Migration:** Free assistance with migrating license keys and database backups when purchasing a new office computer (up to 2 migrations per year).

---

## 2. What Happens if AMC Lapses?

- The CA's WebRajya ERP installation **never stops working**.
- All historical data, clients, invoices, and documents remain 100% accessible locally.
- New statutory update downloads and priority technical support tickets are suspended until AMC is renewed.
- No penalty is charged for late renewal; upon renewing, the system automatically pulls the latest cumulative update package.
